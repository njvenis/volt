# ADR-0003: Configurable governance and portion-level remediation

Status: current product direction.

Ownership supports multiple people and teams. Portions identify the exact affected membership; an authorised confirmation completes only that portion, while a separately authorised whole-group action may cover remaining membership. Risk acceptance is distinct from remediation. Roles, scopes, approval thresholds and separation-of-duties rules are configurable. Authority-changing and assessment actions are audited.
